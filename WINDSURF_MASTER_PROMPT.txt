PAYMENT STATE MACHINE

- pending
- successful
- failed
- reversed

Rules:
- electronic checkout starts pending
- client-side success is never authoritative
- only verified provider status can finalize an electronic payment
- all initiation attempts have idempotency keys

- store masked phone/card details only
